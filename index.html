About Zynex Core

Zynex Core is a B2B AI automation and custom software company. We replace manual, error-prone work with systems that run on their own: AI agents, workflow automation, internal tools, client portals, dashboards and integrations. We stay on after launch with monitoring and support.

AI automation

Chatbots, document processing, lead handling and reporting agents tailored to your data and tools.

Custom software

Web apps, admin panels and APIs built for how your company actually works, not a template.

Infrastructure and care
